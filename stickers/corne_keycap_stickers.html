Corne Choc Pro — all-layer keycap stickers (German · macOS)

One sticker per key shows every layer at once, color-coded by layer. Print on sticker foil at 100% / actual size (turn OFF "fit to page"). Each cell is 13 mm; cut on the dashed line and keep it centered (caps are negatively curved). Home-row modifiers are separate mini-stickers (bottom) for the front edge of the cap.
